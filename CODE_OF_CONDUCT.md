# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Pitwall a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

- Be welcoming, respectful, and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the project.
- No harassment, trolling, personal attacks, or unwanted attention.
- No publishing of others' private information.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to the maintainers (contact to be published with
the public repository). Maintainers will review fairly, may warn, temporarily
ban, or permanently ban contributors, and will respect reporter confidentiality.
